# Ghana Garrison Schools Management System (SMS) - User Guide

Welcome to the **Ghana Garrison Schools Management System**. This guide provides step-by-step instructions to help teachers, administrators, and staff navigate the system efficiently.

---

## 1. System Overview
The Garrison SMS is a specialized administrative portal designed for the Ghana Armed Forces educational directorate. 
**Key Features:**
*   **Command Hub:** Real-time strategic oversight of finances and operations.
*   **Treasury Registry:** Secure management of fees, collections, and arrears.
*   **Personnel Induction:** Professional workflow for applicant registration and admission.
*   **Operations Node:** Tracking student movement (Exeats) and classroom capacity.

---

## 2. Login and Account Setup

### How to Sign In
1.  Navigate to the login URL provided by your administrator.
2.  Enter your **Username** in the first field.
3.  Enter your **Password** in the second field.
4.  Click **Enter Dashboard**.

### Managing Your Profile
1.  Click on your **Avatar/Name** at the bottom of the left sidebar.
2.  Select **Profile** to view your credentials.
3.  Select **Change Password** to update your security settings.

---

## 3. Dashboard Navigation

The Dashboard (Command Hub) is organized into tactical tabs:
*   **Overview**: View "Total Revenue," "Arrears," and "Expenditure" at a glance.
*   **Treasury Intel**: Detailed breakdown of collection performance by category (Levy, Books, etc.).
*   **Operations**: Visual load distribution of classroom slots across your Garrison unit.
*   **Audit Log**: A chronological stream of financial activity.

## 4. Student Management

### Adding a New Applicant (Induction)
1.  Go to **Academics & Operations** > **Registration** > **New Registration**.
2.  **Personnel Info**: Enter the student's legal name, DOB, and contact details.
3.  **Strategic Placement**: Select the target class and Garrison category (SVC/CIV).
4.  **Guardian Node**: Enter parent/guardian credentials.
5.  Click **Register Applicant** to save to the registry.

### Managing Applicants
1.  Go to **Manage Applicants**.
2.  Use the **Search Bar** to find specific candidates.
3.  Select candidates using the checkboxes and click **Migrate (Admit)** to move them into the active student directory.

---

## 5. Academic Records

### Managing Grades
1.  Go to **Academics & Operations** > **Gradebook**.
2.  Select the **Class** and **Subject**.
3.  Input the scores for Continuous Assessment and Exams.
4.  Click **Save Changes** to commit to the central node.

### Generating Reports
1.  Go to **Terminal Reports**.
2.  Select the **Academic Term** and **Student**.
3.  Click **Generate PDF** to produce the official report card.

---

## 6. Payments and Fees

### Creating a Receipt
1.  Go to **Finance & Collections** > **Fee Management** > **Create Receipt**.
2.  Search for the **Student** or **Applicant**.
3.  Select the **Allocation** (e.g., Levy, Uniform, Textbooks).
4.  The system will automatically calculate the price.
5.  Click **Authorize & Synchronize** to generate the official PDF receipt.

> **[Screenshot Placeholder: Create Receipt form with total amount calculation]**

### Tracking Arrears
1.  Go to **Debt Ledger**.
2.  Review the "Balance" column to identify students with outstanding debts.
3.  Use the **Print** button to export a list of arrears for management review.

---

## 7. Communication Tools

### Sending Announcements
1.  Go to **General** > **Announcements**.
2.  Click **Create New Announcement**.
3.  Compose your message and select the target audience (Teachers or Parents).
4.  Click **Broadcast** to send via the integrated SMS/Email gateway.

---

## 8. Troubleshooting and FAQs

**Q: I get a "403 Forbidden" error when trying to view branding.**
*   *Solution*: Ensure you are logged in with an account that has at least "Admin" or "Garrison Director" privileges.

**Q: The system is slow to load data.**
*   *Solution*: Click the **Re-Sync** or **Refresh** button on the page header to force a fresh data fetch from the backend.

**Q: I cannot delete a payment record.**
*   *Solution*: For security reasons, the system prevents the deletion of payments that already have a generated receipt. Contact a Super Admin for ledger adjustments.
